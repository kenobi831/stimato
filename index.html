Free Flooring Estimate Calculator for Contractors

Stimato turns jobsite measurements into a clean, professional flooring estimate in a few minutes. Enter room dimensions, add a waste allowance, list your materials, labor, and removal, then print a PDF or copy the estimate into a text or email for your client.

  • Square footage by room — length × width for each room, or one whole-area total.

Flooring estimate FAQ

How do I calculate flooring square footage?

Measure the length and width of each room in feet and multiply them. A 12 ft × 14 ft room is 168 sq ft. Split L-shaped rooms into rectangles, measure each one, and add them together. Stimato does this for every room you enter and keeps a running total.

How much waste should I add for flooring?

About 8% is common for straight installs of laminate, vinyl plank, and hardwood. Plan for about 12% for diagonal or herringbone layouts, rooms with many angles, or patterned tile. Stimato defaults to 8% and lets you change it per room.

What should a flooring estimate include?

A clear estimate lists the area in square feet, the material, installation labor, removal and disposal of old flooring, prep work such as leveling, trim or transitions, tax, the total, any deposit, and how long the price is valid. Stimato has a field for each one.

Is Stimato really free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it is free and no sign-up is needed. Because nothing is stored by Stimato, print to PDF or copy the text before you close the page.

Can I use it on my phone at the jobsite?

Yes. Stimato is built for phones first. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Get App or your browser's install option.
